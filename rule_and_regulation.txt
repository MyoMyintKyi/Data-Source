SUMMARY:
Mandatory standards for verifying travel document validity, machine-readable
travel documents (MRTD), and entry visa compliance before issuing international
boarding passes.

RULES & SPECIFICATIONS:

Rule ID: GOV-DOC-101
Title: Six-Month Passport Validity Requirement
Statement: Passengers traveling across international borders must present a
passport valid for at least 6 months beyond the scheduled date of departure
from the destination country, unless bilateral treaties explicitly permit
shorter validity periods (e.g., US-Canada, EEA internal transit).
Enforcement Level: BLOCKING_GATE
Exceptions: Diplomatic/Official passports with explicit bilateral exemption
letters on file.

Rule ID: GOV-APIS-102
Title: Advance Passenger Information System (APIS) Transmission
Statement: All international passenger manifests, including full legal name,
date of birth, nationality, gender, passport number, and expiration date, must
be transmitted to border control authorities no later than 60 minutes prior
to departure.
Penalties: Carrier liability fines up to $10,000 USD per non-transmitted or
incorrectly transmitted passenger record.

COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST:

Inspect physical passport for bio-page lamination integrity and watermarks.

Verify machine-readable zone (MRZ) scan matches manual input data exactly.

Check electronic Travel Authorization (eTA/ESTA) status via national portal.

Internal standards governing the storage, masking, and automated purging of
Passenger Name Records (PNR) and sensitive payment information.

POLICY STATEMENTS:

PII Masking & Encryption:
All credit card numbers, payment tokens, passport bio-data, and national ID
credentials must be encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using
AES-256. Primary Account Numbers (PAN) must be masked to display only the
last four digits across all staff-facing dashboards and invoice outputs.

Data Retention & Purging:
Passenger Name Records (PNR) and associated travel itinerary data must be
archived from operational databases 180 days post-travel completion.
Immutable audit logs are retained for 7 years to meet tax audit obligations,
after which all PII is permanently scrubbed.

Right to Erasure (Data Subject Requests):
Customer requests for data erasure must be processed within 30 calendar days,
excluding records currently under active customs investigation, tax audit
retention holds, or pending chargeback disputes.

POLICY STATEMENTS:

Notice and Notification Requirements:
Passengers requiring wheelchair stowage, oxygen concentrators, or specialized
boarding assistance must lodge the request at least 48 hours prior to
scheduled departure. Emergency assistance requests within 48 hours must be
accommodated to the maximum extent operationally viable without delaying
departure schedules.

Service Animals:
Recognized, certified service dogs traveling with individuals with sensory or
physical disabilities must be accommodated in the passenger cabin/saloon
without surcharge. Animals must remain seated at the passenger's feet and
fitted with an approved harness at all times.

Medical Clearance (MEDIF):
Passengers requiring supplemental stretcher arrangements, continuous positive
airway pressure (CPAP) without self-contained battery certs, or individuals
with infectious conditions must submit a Medical Information Form (MEDIF)
signed by a licensed physician within 10 days of travel.
